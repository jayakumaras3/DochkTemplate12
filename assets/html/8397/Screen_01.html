Every conversation you have as a Zydus sales professional carries the weight of a 74-year legacy.  

Zydus was built by Mr. Ramanbhai B. Patel, a first-generation entrepreneur with a simple conviction: that healthcare should reach the people who need it most. That conviction has grown into a global healthcare business spanning formulations, biologics, vaccines and wellness products, which reach patients across continents. 

You are now part of that story. But a legacy this size is only as strong as the person standing in front of a doctor, pharmacist or hospital administrator today. When you walk into a clinic or a chemist’s counter, you are not simply selling a product. You represent a brand, a set of values and a promise made to patients you will never meet. 

This course will introduce you to the fundamentals of that role. You will learn how to carry yourself as a representative of the organisation, how to build genuine engagement with healthcare customers, how to talk about product value in a way that is clear and credible and how to maintain the professional standards that protect both you and the organisation you represent. 

By the end of this course, you should be able to: 

  • Describe the standards of conduct expected when representing the organisation in front of healthcare customers 

  • Apply techniques for engaging healthcare customers professionally and building credible relationships 

  • Communicate product value clearly and compliantly during customer interactions
